# EduAudit Pro 2026: Teaching & Learning Quality Auditor

> **Accreditation-grade Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Internal Quality Audit System**  
> Designed for Internal Academic Quality Auditors, Deans, and Program Coordinators to evaluate the **pedagogical depth and quality** of teaching portfolios, rather than merely ticking off submission checklists.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Going Beyond the Checklist: Deep Teaching Quality Assessment
Most university audits stop at bureaucratic completeness: *"Did the lecturer submit Folder 01 to 13? Yes/No."*  
**EduAudit Pro evaluates the actual quality of teaching and learning** across **6 Quality Pillars**:
1. **Constructive Alignment & Bloom Rigor (25%)**: Checks whether Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) match Program Outcomes (PLOs) and whether assessment tasks truly demand the promised Bloom Taxonomy depth (e.g., C5 Synthesis vs C1 Recall).
2. **Assessment Rigor & Rubric Quality (20%)**: Analyzes whether rubrics provide descriptive criteria across performance tiers (Distinction to Fail) and checks for pre-moderation and post-moderation vetting.
3. **Marking Rigor & Formative Feedback (20%)**: Examines representative student scripts (**3 High, 3 Mid, 3 Low**) to verify that lecturers provide meaningful written feed-forward critique rather than solely raw numbers.
4. **CQI Actionability & Closing the Loop (15%)**: Evaluates whether the lecturer's self-reflection diagnoses pedagogical root causes and whether proposed improvements are concrete, measurable, and tracked from the previous semester.
5. **Curriculum Currency & Learning Resources (10%)**: Checks reference recency (< 5 years) and instructional material clarity.
6. **Student Voice & Academic Governance (10%)**: Monitors attendance thresholds (flagging <80% attendance anomalies) and responsiveness to student course ratings.

---

### 2. Effortless Folder & Google Drive Management (Non-Coder Friendly)
- **Upload Local Folder**: Drag & drop or browse any local Teaching File folder from your computer. The app auto-categorizes files into the 13 folders automatically.
- **Upload ZIP Archive**: Drop a `.zip` archive; EduAudit extracts and analyzes the contents client-side.
- **Google Drive Integration**: Connect directly to your course folder on Google Drive (`drive.google.com`).
- **Ready-to-Deploy Google Apps Script**: Included script template for your Google Sheets to automate folder generation and sync audit findings.

---

### 3. Executive Dashboard & Formal Audit Report
- **Overall Quality Index (%)** & Star Rating (Exemplary, Commendable, Satisfactory, Quality Risk).
- **Quality vs. Submission Comparison Card**: Side-by-side view contrasting submission compliance with pedagogical quality.
- **Constructive Alignment Matrix**: CLO attainment tracking against cohort targets.
- **Corrective Action Requests (CAR) Tracker**: Logs Critical Non-Compliances (CNC), Minor Deficiencies (MD), and Opportunities for Improvement (OFI).
- **One-Click Formal Internal Audit Report**: Printable, accreditation-ready report with institutional header, signature blocks, and action plan.
